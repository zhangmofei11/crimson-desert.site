Recommended Builds

Beginner Build - Longsword

  • • Weapon: Iron Longsword +5
  • • Playstyle: Balanced, focus on fundamentals
  • • Skills: Whirlwind Strike, Power Throw
  • • Tips: Learn grab attacks early

Damage Build - Greatsword

  • • Weapon: Obsidian Greatsword +7
  • • Playstyle: Heavy hits, stance breaks
  • • Skills: Earth Shatter, Dragon Rush
  • • Tips: Time charged attacks carefully

Speed Build - Dual Blades

  • • Weapon: Serpent Dual Blades +6
  • • Playstyle: Aggressive, maintain pressure
  • • Skills: Whirlwind Strike, Berserker Rage
  • • Tips: Chain combos without stopping

Boss Hunter - Spear + Bow

  • • Weapons: Wyvern Spear +5, Hunter Bow +5
  • • Playstyle: Ranged dominance, safe damage
  • • Skills: Dragon Rush, Iron Will
  • • Tips: Keep distance, punish from afar
