Master the art of war in the desert

🏰 SIEGE WARFARE

Siege Preparation

  • • Scout enemy defenses first
  • • Cut off supply routes
  • • Build siege camps outside range
  • • Deploy shields against arrows

Breaching

  • • Use catapults on walls
  • • Create ladder points
  • • Send expendable units first
  • • Maintain supply lines

Defense Tips

  • • Place archers on walls
  • • Keep reserves inside
  • • Oil cauldrons on gates
  • • Have retreat routes planned

Siege Equipment

  • • Catapults - Wall damage
  • • Battering Rams - Gate breach
  • • Siege Towers - Safe wall entry
  • • Greek Fire - Area denial

🗺️ CONQUEST MODE

Phase 1: Scout

  • • Identify key targets
  • • Map enemy positions
  • • Plan supply routes

Phase 2: Isolate

  • • Capture surrounding areas
  • • Cut off reinforcements
  • • Starve enemy supplies

Phase 3: Strike

  • • Concentrate forces
  • • Execute siege
  • • Hold and consolidate

💡 BATTLE TIPS

Terrain

Use dunes for ambushes. Control high ground for archery advantage.

Weather

Sandstorms reduce visibility. Plan accordingly for night attacks.

Morale

Keep units fed and paid. Low morale causes desertion.
