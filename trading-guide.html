  • • Border Town → Capital (Horses)

🟡 Safe Routes

  • • Farmland → Mountain (Wheat)

💰 ECONOMY TIPS

Buy Low, Sell High

  • • Check prices in multiple cities
  • • Buy when supply is high (harvest season)
  • • Sell when demand peaks (festival season)
  • • Watch for regional shortages

Reduce Costs

  • • Buy your own camels early
  • • Upgrade caravan guards gradually
  • • Avoid toll roads when possible
  • • Join merchant guild for discounts

Avoid Risks

  • • Check for bandit activity
  • • Travel in caravan groups
  • • Insure high-value cargo
  • • Keep emergency gold reserves

Scale Up

  • • Unlock better trade routes
  • • Build your own trading post
  • • Hire merchant companions
  • • Invest in trade skills early
