Mounts & Pets

All mounts, pets, and companions in Crimson Desert.

Mounts

🐴

Horses

Basic mount. Fast travel and exploration essential.

• Cost: 1,000 Silver
• Speed: Fast
• Availability: Chapter 1 Stable
🏇

War Horses

Combat-trained horses. Can attack while mounted.

• Cost: 10,000 Silver
• Speed: Very Fast
• Special: Trample attack
🐪

Camels

Desert terrain specialist. Best in arid regions.

• Cost: 5,000 Silver
• Speed: Medium
• Special: Desert endurance
🐉

Wyvern (Dragon)

Epic flying mount. Essential for late game and final boss.

• Cost: Story Mission (Ch4)
• Speed: Extremely Fast (Flying)
• Special: Aerial combat, fire breath

Pets & Companions

🐕

War Hound

Combat companion. Attacks nearby enemies.

How to get: Complete Chapter 2 mission
🦅

Scout Eagle

Marks enemies and treasures on minimap.

How to get: Purchase from Hunter's Guild
🐈

Lucky Cat

Increases silver and item drop rates.

How to get: Rare drop from bosses
🦊

Swift Fox

Increases mounted movement speed.

How to get: Complete hunting challenge
🦉

Night Owl

Night vision and reduced enemy detection.

How to get: Complete stealth missions
🐉

Baby Dragon

Rare pet. Provides fire resistance and damage aura.

How to get: Complete all dragon-related missions
