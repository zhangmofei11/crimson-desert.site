Combat Guide

Master Crimson Desert's unique freeform combat system combining swordplay with wrestling techniques.

Freeform Combat System

Crimson Desert features a unique "freeform" combat system that combines traditional swordplay with dynamic wrestling moves. Unlike typical action RPGs, you can grab enemies, throw them, perform suplexes, and chain aerial attacks.

Core Combat Actions

  • • Light Attack - Fast sword strikes
  • • Heavy Attack - Powerful charged strikes
  • • Grab (G) - Catch enemies and perform throws
  • • Guard (H) - Block incoming attacks
  • • Dodge - Quick movement to avoid attacks

Advanced Techniques

  • • Suplex - Grab + Heavy while running
  • • Aerial Attack - Jump + Heavy for airborne strikes
  • • Throw Counter - Grab during enemy attack
  • • Chain Combo - String attacks into grapples

Combat Skills

Whirlwind Strike

Spin attack hitting all nearby enemies. Great for crowds.

SP Cost: 15 | Cooldown: 8s

Power Throw

Charged throw that launches enemy into other enemies.

SP Cost: 20 | Cooldown: 12s

Dragon Rush

Dash forward and strike. Stuns on hit.

SP Cost: 25 | Cooldown: 15s

Earth Shatter

Slam ground creating shockwave. Knocks down enemies.

SP Cost: 30 | Cooldown: 20s

Iron Will

Temporary invincibility. Perfect for boss attacks.

SP Cost: 35 | Cooldown: 30s

Berserker Rage

Increased damage and speed for 20 seconds.

SP Cost: 40 | Cooldown: 45s

Recommended Combos

Starter Combo

Light × 3 → Heavy → Grab → Throw

Safe opening combo for most enemies.

Crowd Control

Whirlwind → Earth Shatter → Heavy × 2

Handle groups of enemies efficiently.

Burst Damage

Dragon Rush → Aerial → Suplex → Heavy

Maximum damage combo for bosses.

Counter Attack

Guard → Grab Counter → Power Throw

Punish enemies who attack into your guard.
