Boss Fight Tips

Study Patterns

Watch enemy attacks for a full cycle before engaging. Every boss has readable patterns.

Use Grabs

Grab attacks break enemy stance and deal bonus damage. Essential for armored bosses.

Mount Benefits

Dragon mount provides massive damage boost in phase 3. Unlock it before final boss.
